## Current scope and acceptance boundary

The active public corpus is **all actual Weather services and their interactions**.
Verified service names are Forecasts, Places and Dashboard. The user requested
four services, but a fourth has not been identified; it is a clarification gap,
not an invented service or a verified four-service system.
The surfaces are OpenAPI, HTTP endpoints, Kafka and scheduled jobs. A separate
restricted service remains local; no private identity, source, endpoint,
configuration, log, screenshot or machine path is included here.

**N5 is a Forecasts-only result.** It is not a pass for the other Weather
services, cross-service behavior, a restricted deployment, or transfer generally.
The remaining actual services and interactions are newly requested work, not an
accomplished result in this snapshot. The latest requested clarity target is
20/20; do not relabel N5's historical 17/20 as that target being met.

RealWorld, GraphQL and Petclinic were removed from active research by the
2026-10-30 scope correction. Their retained measurements and unsuccessful
attempts remain historical evidence. Deleted raw RealWorld artifacts were not
reconstructed for this record. Those failures do not by themselves reject the
corrected active scope, and excluding a corpus does not convert its failure into
a success.

The current cost allowance is **net tokens <=1.5x comparable same-service Direct**,
conditional on complete, truthful and clear documentation and transfer. Time is
not a gate. The Forecasts Direct comparator is 170,482 net; N5 is 236,792 net
(1.389x). Reuse their immutable measurements instead of rerunning them simply
because a policy changed. The Direct comparator itself had partial meanings
under the terminal rubric; its cost is not proof of perfect reference quality.

## Conclusions that survive the experiments

1. Exact evidence, valid citations, complete event inventories and successful
   export do not prove that reader prose explains the consequences of the code.
   Mechanical and semantic acceptance must remain separate.
2. Smaller initial prompts, fewer tool bytes or a compact sidecar do not imply
   lower complete author cost. Repairs, context recovery, finalization and
   purposeful rereads count; critic and engineering costs remain visible too.
3. An author must preserve alternatives, local outcomes, failure boundaries and
   resource meaning when first writing prose. A symbol, citation or checklist
   mention is not that explanation. Retrospective loss traces locate a problem;
   they are not prospective evidence that a new instruction fixes it.
4. Source-first compact views can keep code readable while moving hashes, byte
   ranges, JVM signatures and origin enums into provenance sidecars. Never obtain
   compactness by deleting branches, nested calls, owners, tests or resources.
5. Native source recovery and strict identity checks solve real acquisition
   defects. They do not justify filling missing immutable evidence from an
   unchecked checkout or presenting lexical relationships as compiler/runtime
   proof.
6. A frozen successful treatment is a bundle of runtime, helper/skill, source,
   renderer and instructions. A newer official binary is not an equivalent
   experimental treatment. Verify the acquisition route before a paid transfer.
7. A candidate can pass one fixed rubric and remain inadequate as the product's
   public face. Keep visual limitations, text-density concerns, service scope and
   improved future targets explicit.

This record indexes retained experience without publishing raw sessions, prompts
or private data. It deliberately states [missing evidence](coverage.md); metadata
coverage is not a claim that every historical result has been fully rescored.
