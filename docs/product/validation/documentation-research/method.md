# Reading the record and designing the next trial

This guidance applies to costly documentation research and qualification.
Ordinary product edits keep the repository's normal focused development loop;
this record introduces no general approval or attestation barrier.

Before a new paid comparison, consult [the index](README.md), locate the nearest
failed and successful treatments, and identify the **untested material delta**.
State which observed failure it could change and what result would falsify the
hypothesis. A renamed workflow or extra instruction is not automatically a new
mechanism. Reuse immutable successful source, baseline and rendering evidence
when their admitted inputs and treatment are unchanged.

## Preserve identity and scope

Record source revision and admitted file/resource scope; runtime/package, helper
and skill commits/hashes; model/reasoning and instructions; renderer; delivery
and author/reviewer phases. Unknown identity or timing remains unavailable. A
runtime's version string alone is insufficient. Verify the supported acquisition
route before a paid author run. Respect an explicitly selected experimental
treatment instead of replacing it with the latest official release.

A full service question includes consequential configuration, contracts,
resources and tests as evidence. Documenting every test operation is not a reader
goal. Paging, source selection for a bounded fetch, compact display and optional
review never silently redefine that question or permit pruning difficult branches.
An accepted service does not qualify other services or their interactions.

## Author and evaluation boundaries

For the later single-author regime, the same Luna Max author generates and
self-reviews until READY. Freeze the result, then let independent critics score
it without leaking either arm's cost, identity or the other output. Preserve
source/provenance clues needed to judge claims. Record disclosure limitations;
blind packaging is not perfect proof of blindness.

Do not repair the measured result after terminal feedback. A later materially
different trial is separate. Earlier initial/feedback/repair protocols remain
distinct historical regimes; their full repair cost stays charged. A stopped
run is not READY, and report-only closeout is not completed semantic work.

Check every mandatory meaning, material false assertion, clarity, navigation and
required visual behavior. Preserve critic disagreement and unavailable visual
coverage. Runtime tests, source identity, export checks and event counts answer
different questions. Do not replace missing evidence with a success label.

The whole-Weather and later Forecasts statement diagnostic are separate scopes,
not matched treatment arms. Do not compare whole-system author cost with the
Forecasts-only Direct baseline. Keep different critic inventories separate,
including the diagnostic's 34 and 40 items; their scores are not observations
on a common denominator. A targeted diagnostic does not qualify the system.

Distinguish review availability from a change in prose: a guard/effect already
present in the first draft cannot demonstrate that a later statement projection
caused it. Keep unresolved omissions after review, language compliance and
rendered literal fidelity visible. An angle-bracket placeholder lost as HTML is
an output defect even when the original Markdown retained the characters.
These negative results justify bounded source/delivery diagnosis, not another
unchanged prompt trial, automatic candidate adoption or release qualification.

For staged v2, keep three claims separate: the sealed inputs match the admitted
helper, all nine staged carriers reached visible tool outputs, and the author
interpreted their meaning correctly. Only the first two were confirmed. Carrier
delivery does not prove that a prepared inventory includes every required file;
the host-registration gap remains under diagnosis. A source-order contradiction
in delivered retry logic is a separate confirmed finding. Do not infer a common
root cause for both observations.

The 433,960 versus 303,490 net comparison is descriptive author cost between two
failed whole-system runs, not an advantage result or a single-factor estimate.
Staging, helper and contract evidence changed together. Preserve the 36- and
33-item critic inventories separately, and do not substitute the Forecasts-only
Direct comparator. Independent helper/proof acceptance and intended mechanical
refusals do not establish semantic quality, authorize another paid author or
lift the quality hold.

For compact presentations, verify display labels against native identifiers in
the entire delivered context. A sidecar can preserve distinct identities while
the reading surface still has a namespace collision. Preserve the rejected
version and revised byte measurement; exact original citation IDs may avoid a
new resolver, but a static projection is not an author integration result.

For a local revision episode, separate source identity from what a paraphrase
actually entails. Preserve exact-source evidence versus author inference and
record observed changes, not hypothetical prevented errors. Keep a root event
audit's chronology separate from a critic who intentionally did not read that
audit. Two supported changes without new material false claims do not qualify
unchanged prose or the complete document. A non-held-out, unpaired diagnostic
cannot supply a causal or token advantage; do not repair it after critique.

For acquisition measurements, distinguish SOURCE transport from unchanged
declaration-proof commands and shared fixture preparation. A full-row batch is
not a part-stream receipt, and display union after retrieval cannot save first
reads. Preserve every selected native binding and explicit omissions. Compare
source text and source maps separately from generic rendered prose; different
test wording cannot establish reader-text byte equality. Warm command counts and
elapsed observations do not supply model-token or semantic-quality evidence.

## Accounting and privacy

Reconcile actual response usage, including in-flight usage settled after a stop.
Keep author generation, self-review, repairs and finalization together. Record
critic, engineering, orchestration and research costs separately; absence from
the production comparison does not make them free. Do not count reasoning twice.
Cached tokens and elapsed intervals are not independently causal savings.

Measure complete tool/prompt/context carriers and actual model usage. A counterfactual
format, reduced initial prompt, file on disk or smaller output has no demonstrated
model-token effect until a comparable prospective run measures it. Do not charge
a whole local sidecar as model input without an actual delivery. Preserve failed
attempts, interruptions, amendments and accounting alongside the final result.

Public records use safe aggregate evidence and stable experiment/product commit
IDs. Retain private source, service/organization/person identities, endpoint and
configuration names, paths, credentials, prompts, logs and screenshots locally.
If no safe summary exists, publish only that detail is withheld. Never restore
deleted raw artifacts merely to make a retrospective look complete.
