## Capture, contracts and privacy-safe diagnosis

The source/syntax route made documentation available without requiring a working
application build; compiler evidence remained a separate authority. Java generated
interfaces, nested build roots, analyzer compatibility and failure diagnostics
were genuine admission concerns. Missing generated inputs cannot justify claiming
that a public entrypoint does not exist. Root/scope changes require renewed
identity checks rather than reusing evidence under a misleading scope label.

`architecture-contract-research-*`, `interaction-work-delivery-*`,
`http-method-navigation-*`, `source-links-render-*` and related contract/route
studies separated discovered HTTP operations from declared OpenAPI contracts,
source-backed declarations, lexical calls and runtime activation. A declared
external relationship can support navigation and diagrams without proving
runtime destination, delivery or persistence. Keep unresolved operations explicit.

Consultations about render limits, source URL defaults, note entrypoints and
compiler failures were bounded reproductions or design feedback. They were not
new author quality benchmarks. A failed required limit or admission check must
remain a failure, not a successful workaround that discards content.

Support reports can retain useful metadata and authorized cached source fragments
without exporting the full source repository. Old failures cannot recover compiler
stderr or original bytes that were never retained. Private-case specifics and
identity mappings remain local; this record preserves only generic lessons.

## Human reading and authoring mechanics

Public Weather reader investigations identified a recurring distinction: the
author can read familiar source code, while byte ranges, origin enums, JVM
signatures, raw IDs and repeated inventories can overwhelm the reading surface.
Compact source, brief consequential gaps and small navigation structures should
retain original code and precise drill-down in sidecars. An auxiliary view must
not invent body roles, execution order or successful external effects.

The static compact-display study preserved three shared bodies while keeping
all source bindings distinct. Its first alias namespace collided with nine
native source references and was rejected. A disjoint namespace passed independent
reconstruction at 6.09% fewer display bytes; retaining original citations instead
gave 5.77% fewer bytes without a new resolver. Neither treatment established model
token savings, author/export integration or improved prose. Keep navigation
namespaces unambiguous in the complete delivered context, not only the sidecar.

Overviews, process steps, nearby failure explanations, collapsible details,
Markdown in secondary sections, correct return labels, field tables, declared
external diagrams and page queues each had separate implementation/reader
checks. A static source/content acceptance, a rendered page and a desktop visual
pass are different evidence. Mobile or uninspected interactions remain unknown.

Authoring mechanics evolved through automatic evidence packets, grouped source,
original-fragment composition, literal repair deltas, checked-page finalization,
supported export checks and one-handoff finalization. The repeated failed
comparisons in [history.md](history.md) show why a smaller first carrier is not
the endpoint: revision, repair, reinspection and binding preparation can dominate
later cost. Preserve valid original prose rather than regenerating it from a
stale copy. Successful old checks cannot certify an incomplete repair.

`native-stdout-authoring-route-v1` was superseded when restrictive experimental
bans were removed; no product code change was required. Avoid turning an
experimental restriction into a missing product feature. The separate editor
arm did not complete cheaply enough and was not evidence for a production
multi-author repair workflow.

The staged Weather v2 mechanical diagnosis retained four intended refusals:
replacement metadata without a previous checked map, a missing rendered target,
a duplicate HTML ID, and unnecessary replacement declarations for unchanged
bindings. Source claim IDs and renderer anchors are different namespaces; prose
edits alone do not imply source rebinding. No helper defect was established and
no causal token/time price was assigned to these cases. Possible guidance gaps
do not justify weakening exact-delta or export checks.

## Semantic loss and future intervention

The Forecasts and retired transfer traces repeatedly found delivered source
meaning that did not survive the first prose. Examples at a generic level are
different create/update rules, blank versus empty input, in-memory versus
persisted values, unsupported-mode errors and independently conditioned effects.
These are not interchangeable outcomes. A row inventory, name, citation, coverage
note or smaller source carrier cannot replace explaining their consequences.

Retrospective positive controls and meaning-intake proposals locate possible
intervention points. They do not establish that a new prompt, deterministic
explanation block, semantic map or checklist improves a future same-author run.
The v3 retired transfer improved some coverage while still costing more and
failing quality. Preserve that negative result; do not keep repeating an
unchanged hypothesis under new workflow names.

The [whole-Weather trial and separate statement diagnostic](history.md#weather-system-and-statement-review-negative-results)
now preserve additional negative evidence. Delivered SQL conditions did not
guarantee complete prose. In the diagnostic, the guard and independent outbox
effect were present before the new review, while same-revision deduplication
remained absent afterwards. Row conservation and source availability therefore
do not establish meaning coverage or isolate a causal benefit of 44 anchors.
The English-for-Russian task failure and lost Markdown status placeholder also
show that source/provenance checks alone do not certify the requested output.

Staged whole-system v2 adds another confirmed delivered-source failure: all
nine initial carriers were fully visible, but the reader contradicted the
connected source's retry assignment before the HTTP send. A 1,332-file seal and
exact 71-file helper identity establish input consistency, not complete selected
evidence or correct interpretation. The missing host-registration input remains
under diagnosis; do not collapse that open delivery question into the separately
confirmed contradiction. Staging, helper and retained-contract input changed
together, so neither the 43.0% author-cost increase nor any coverage difference
isolates an individual mechanism. Statement rows and generic checklists still
have no demonstrated guarantee of reader quality.

The bounded Dashboard local-revision diagnostic supplied two source-supported
narrowings at 51,874 net, with no observed new material false assertion in those
changes. It also exposed an inaccurate source-basis paraphrase and raw citation
IDs in human prose. Recording a source rationale does not itself establish
entailment. Root observed the notes before application; the independent critic
did not inspect chronology and correctly retained that limit. The episode
does not certify unchanged content, catch a demonstrably unsupported new claim,
establish error-prevention causality or compare token cost with another method.

The next justified step is bounded diagnosis of the confirmed source/delivery
and prose losses. A new whole-system qualification needs a concrete material
delta, exact admitted treatment and the full fixed question. A new engine,
semantic prepass, reviewer model, persistent delivery ledger or broad planner is
not authorized merely by this retrospective. The quality hold remains; these
failures do not authorize merge, full CI, packaging or publication.
