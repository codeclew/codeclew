## Verified properties

- The graph has one dependency order and an explicit stop path.
- Every core step has a practical reason, bounded scope, focused verification,
  a non-contradiction argument, and an objective Definition of Done.
- Thread, thread-context, contract fact-set, and query-index authorities are
  separate and immutable.
- Exact contract claims require exact provider and consumer artifacts.
- The OpenAPI surface, structural rule IDs, global limits, and failure behavior
  are closed before implementation.
- The read-only boundary does not imply candidate execution, behavioral
  compatibility, coordinated publication, or mutation support.
- The same ten tasks are frozen before implementation and reused for the final
  Default-versus-Codeclew adoption decision under equal, explicit caps.
- Secondary features are recorded with promotion evidence but are not hidden
  prerequisites of the core path.
