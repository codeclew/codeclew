The operating model

A narrow path from intent to verified change.

Every step retains its authority. Unknowns remain obligations instead of being polished into confidence.

01

Open exact context

Bind a clean repository, target ref, language, compilation, and intent to an immutable session.

clew change open …
02

Prepare in isolation

Validate a closed edit plan and compile or test the candidate in a managed worktree.

clew change prepare …
03

Publish explicitly

Recheck freshness, review evidence and obligations, then move the ref with compare-and-swap.

clew change publish …
